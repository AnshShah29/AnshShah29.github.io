Hello!

I am an architecture student driven by the intersection of sustainability, material innovation, and community-centered design. My work explores how architecture can respond to environmental challenges while creating meaningful social spaces. Through projects ranging from conservation-based urban infill to interdisciplinary sustainability hubs, I investigate how ecological systems, performance strategies, and parametric tools can shape thoughtful, responsive environments. Proficient in Rhino, Grasshopper, D5 Render, and the Adobe Suite, I use digital workflows to test form, performance, and spatial experience. I am particularly interested in regenerative materials, adaptive reuse, and architecture that balances technical rigor with social and environmental responsibility. My goal is to contribute to design practices that prioritize long-term resilience, innovation, and impact.
