01 / Statement

Architecture is not neutral. Every threshold, material, and shadow is a decision - one that shapes how people move, feel, and belong. My work begins with ecology and ends with human life.

02 / About

Architecture rooted in place, ecology, and human life.

I am an architectural design student based in Ottawa and Toronto, completing my Bachelor of Architectural Studies at Carleton University's Azrieli School of Architecture.

My practice moves between ecological thinking and institutional design - from wildlife habitat structures in the Dominion Arboretum to community music schools along the Rideau corridor.
